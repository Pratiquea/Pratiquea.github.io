I am a PhD candidate in the Robotic Workers Lab at the University of Nevada, Reno, advised by Prof. Christos Papachristos. My research focuses on developing resilient autonomous robotic systems that can overcome mobility and manipulation constraints.

Before my PhD, I completed my Masters in Robotics at the University of Maryland, College Park, where I was a member of the Perception and Robotics Group (PRG) advised by Prof. Yiannis Aloimonos and worked on autonomous vision-based navigation for quadrotors. Earlier, I was a Research Associate at IIIT-Delhi with Dr. P.B. Sujit and Dr. Sanjit Kaul on a self-driving car project for Indian urban scenarios.

In my spare time, I fly my own custom-built FPV drone and go on hikes.
